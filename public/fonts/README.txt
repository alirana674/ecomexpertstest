Place licensed Gilroy font files here to enable the design's confirmed fonts
(see src/styles/fonts.css for the prepared @font-face declarations):

- Gilroy-Bold.woff2          (mobile "Let's get started!" heading)
- Gilroy-Regular.woff2       (body)
- Gilroy-Medium.woff2        (step labels, product card descriptions)
- Gilroy-SemiBold.woff2      (step titles)
- Gilroy-RegularItalic.woff2 ("Save my system for later" link)

Gilroy is a commercial typeface and is NOT bundled with this repository.
Montserrat (Google Fonts) is used as the documented fallback.
